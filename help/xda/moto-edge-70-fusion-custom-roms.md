# Moto Edge 70 Fusion: any custom ROMs yet?

XDA post: https://xdaforums.com/posts/90777403/

Not yet, and nobody's given a timeline. LineageOS doesn't list the Edge 70 Fusion on their device wiki, so there's no official build.

It also comes in two versions, Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 internationally and 7s Gen 4 in India, so a dev would need to own one and build a device tree for it first.

Don't flash builds from other Moto models in the meantime. LineageOS themselves say nearly identical devices aren't enough, and you'd most likely end up with a bootloop.
